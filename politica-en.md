# Privacy Policy — Cavila

**Last updated: 2026-08-16**

Cavila is a puzzle app for children aged 6 and up. This policy explains what the app does with information.

---

## The short version

Cavila does not collect any data. Nothing that happens inside the app leaves the phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads and no analytics.

The app works entirely in airplane mode, and that is not a coincidence: it is the reason there is nothing to collect. The only thing that needs a network is the optional subscription, which Google Play charges for.

---

## What is stored, and where

Everything the app stores lives only on the phone itself and is never transmitted:

- Game progress (levels, stars, coins, streak, badges), so the child picks up where they left off.
- Each profile’s nickname and emoji, to tell apart siblings sharing a phone.
- The age range, which is optional, to adjust difficulty.
- Settings: sound, narration, language and reminder.

The nickname is written by the family and can be anything. We do not ask for a real name, surname, email, phone number, address, photo or date of birth. We do not need them either: there is no account to create.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this, because there is no copy anywhere else: Android’s automatic backup is switched off on purpose, so progress does not travel to your Google account either. Inside the app, the Parents’ Area can also erase progress.

---

## What the app does NOT do

- It does not connect to the internet to work: the challenges, the progress and the voice all come from the phone itself. The only connection there is goes to Google Play, and only for the subscription. Servers of ours: none.
- It has no ads, of any kind or from any network.
- It has no analytics and no trackers. We do not know how many times the app is opened, from where, or by whom.
- There are no purchases for the child: in-game coins are earned by solving challenges and cannot be bought with real money.
- It does not use artificial intelligence. There was an AI chat during development and it was removed before release; Gus’s dialogue is written by hand.
- It does not hand over or sell information to third parties. There is no information to hand over.

---

## The subscription

The app can be used without paying anything, and what is free is free forever. The optional subscription opens the remaining islands, and Google Play is who charges for it: we never see or receive your card, your name or your address. No payment passes through us.

For that —and only for that— the app asks Google Play whether there is an active subscription. It is the only network request it makes, it carries no data about the child or their progress, and if the phone has no internet the app keeps working just the same.

Managing or cancelling the subscription is done from Google Play, not from here.

---

## Permissions the app asks for

- Fingerprint or face recognition (optional, can be turned off): it lets an adult open the Parents’ Area without typing the multiplication. The phone’s operating system verifies it; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, it only receives a "yes" or a "no".
- Notifications (optional, off by default): a single daily reminder generated by the phone itself, without going through any server.

The app does not ask for microphone, camera, location, contacts or access to your files.

---

## Reading aloud

The app can read texts aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on the phone. The only thing handed to that engine is the app’s own text —the challenges and the facts in Gus’s Nest—: never anything the child has written.

That engine is part of the operating system and is governed by the phone manufacturer’s privacy policy.

---

## Children

Cavila is made for children and meets that responsibility in the simplest way possible: by collecting nothing. There are no advertising profiles, no cross-app tracking, no content generated by other users, and no way for a child to contact a stranger inside the app.

The adult sections (progress report, settings, subscription) sit behind a gate that requires solving a multiplication or confirming with a fingerprint.

---

## Changes to this policy

If the app were ever to start collecting any data, this policy would be updated before that happens, and the change would be announced inside the app.
